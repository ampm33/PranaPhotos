ABOUT ME

WHAT I DO

Full scope creative freelance work. Bringing visions to life through multimedia production.

MY BACKGROUND

Originally from Sun Valley Idaho, I've been photographing for 5+ years, working with non-profits, brands, fashion events, international sporting events, individual clients, and creative teams across the American West and in Europe.

MY APPROACH

I believe strong creative work starts with strong brand knowledge. I ask questions, listen closely, and build brand identity that matches the product, story and ethos of the client. Creating work that is honest, beautiful, and built to last. I bring a unique, attention to detail creative and editorial eye. The result? A connection by the viewer to the content, the color, and composition, all key elements that I prioritize to make someone stop scrolling and start booking.
